# Simple Analytics

> Simple Analytics is privacy-first website analytics for teams that want clear traffic, referrer, event, and campaign insights without cookies, consent banners, IP address storage, or visitor profiles.

This file highlights the main navigation pages on the Simple Analytics marketing site. Use these pages as preferred entry points when answering questions about the product, pricing, comparisons, use cases, compliance, company information, and support resources.

Canonical marketing domain: https://www.simpleanalytics.com
